How to use Classify

🔑
Your Key — A unique code identifying your schedule. Save it to sync across devices.
📅
Add Classes — Use the Search tab or tap +. Pick multiple days at once.
📝
Add Exams — Go to Exams tab and tap +. Countdowns start automatically.
🔍
Course Search — Search the AIU course database and auto-fill your schedule.
🔔
Reminders — Enable notifications in Settings to get alerts before class/exam.
↩️
Undo — Accidentally deleted something? Tap Undo in the popup that appears.
